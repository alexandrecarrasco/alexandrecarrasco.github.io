This blog is where I write about whatever is on my mind: things I tried, what I learned from them, and what I found while researching. Right now that is mostly identity and access, and AI agents in particular. When I write about standards, I use the official documents and say when something is still a draft.

No hype, no sales pitch. Just someone learning in public and sharing notes. If something here helps you, or if you think I got it wrong, I'd love to hear about it.

*Opinions are my own.*
